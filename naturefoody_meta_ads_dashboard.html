🚨 Performance Drop Alerts — Watch Out For These Right Now
🔴

URGENT — Set Up Meta Conversions API (CAPI)

Relying only on Shopify Pixel? You're missing 20–40% of actual conversions due to iOS privacy restrictions. Go to Shopify Admin → Apps → Meta → Enable Conversions API. Do this before spending another rupee on ads.

⚠️

Ad Fatigue — Refresh Creatives Every 10–12 Days

Meta's 2026 algorithm shows ads more aggressively in short bursts. If the same creative runs past 12 days, your CPM rises sharply and CTR drops. Build a creative rotation calendar now.

🟡

Broad Targeting Is Losing Power

Interest-based targeting (e.g. "Ayurveda," "organic skincare") is now mostly a suggestion to Meta's AI — not a hard filter. Shift budget toward Advantage+ audience settings instead of manual interest stacking.

🟡

Free Shipping / Gift Offer Not Visible Enough

Your ₹999 free shipping and ₹1,200 free gift are powerful purchase triggers — but likely invisible in current ads. Make these the headline of at least one active campaign. Many brands lose sales simply from low offer awareness.

🔵

Summer Season Approaching — Activate Sunscreen Campaigns

April–June Indian summer = peak demand for SPF products. Your Cream Gel SPF 50 PA++++ should be front-and-center starting this week. Bundle with Aloe Vera Gel for a "Summer Skin Protection Duo" campaign.

🟢

WhatsApp Status Ads — Opportunity to Capture Now

New placement live this month. Indian consumers are deeply active on WhatsApp. Natural beauty brands who activate WhatsApp Status placements now will get lower CPMs before competition heats up.

🟢

Eye Care Sub-Category is Underserved — Move Fast

Under-eye concerns (dark circles, puffiness) are exploding on Instagram Reels in India. Your Eye Radiance 360 Cream & Under Eye Serum face very low competition from indie natural brands in this niche.

🎨 Creative Trends — What's Working in Indian Skincare Right Now
🎥
#1 — UGC (User-Generated Content) Videos

Raw, authentic phone-shot videos of real customers. No studio needed. A customer applying Face Glow Serum saying "My skin looks brighter in 2 weeks" outperforms any polished production.

36.8% share of winning ads

💡 For Nature Foody: Brief 3 existing customers to film 30-second testimonials for Face Serum, Hair Oil & Brightening Serum.

📸
#2 — Before & After with Skin Texture Close-Ups

62% of top-performing skincare ads pair UGC with visual proof. Dull skin close-up → bright healthy skin after using your Brightening Serum. Raw and real beats polished every time.

62% of top skincare ads

💡 Use the Brightening Water Serum: "Visible results in 4 weeks" claim + before/after photo series.

🏭
#3 — Founder Story / Handmade Process Videos

Show the founder or team preparing products. Show raw farm-grown ingredients. Show the handcrafting process from Kanchipuram. Builds massive trust — especially in India where chemical-brand skepticism is rising.

Highest brand trust builder

💡 "Made in Kanchipuram with love — no chemicals, just nature." Film a 45-second process reel.

🎯
#4 — Single Hero Benefit Ads

Don't list 5 benefits in one ad. Pick ONE. "Reduces dark spots in 4 weeks." One claim, one creative, one CTA. These run 3× longer and convert 40% better than multi-claim ads.

3× longer run time

💡 Face Glow Serum ad: "Bright complexion in 4 weeks. Naturally." Just that — nothing else.

⏱️
#5 — Problem Hook in First 3 Seconds

Start your Reel with a direct problem statement. "Fed up of dark spots that won't go?" or "Hair falling out every morning?" Lead with pain, then offer the solution. You have 3 seconds to stop the scroll.

Critical — 3 second rule

💡 Hair Growth Oil hook: "Noticed your hairline moving back? 🚨 This fixed mine in 6 weeks."
